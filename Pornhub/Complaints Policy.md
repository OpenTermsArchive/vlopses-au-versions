Australian eSafety
==================

**Information for Australian users**

This information is for Australian users. It explains our online safety measures, how to report content or raise a compliance complaint, and how to contact the eSafety Commissioner or independent support services.

**The role of the eSafety Commissioner**

The eSafety Commissioner (eSafety) is Australia’s independent regulator for online safety. Its functions under the Online Safety Act 2021 include administering online safety schemes, industry codes and standards, and providing education and guidance to help Australians stay safe online. Further information is available on [eSafety’s website](https://www.esafety.gov.au/).

**Illegal and age restricted material**

Australia’s online safety framework distinguishes between categories of illegal and age-restricted material. In summary:

*   Class 1A includes child sexual exploitation, pro-terror, and extreme crime and violence material.
*   Class 1B includes certain crime, violence and drug-related material.
*   Class 1C includes certain refused-classification material depicting or describing specific fetish practices or fantasies.
*   Class 2 includes adult material classified, or likely to be classified, X18+ or R18+, and corresponding restricted publications. It includes pornography and certain other high-impact material.

These are summaries, not the full legal definitions. Further information is available through [eSafety’s Online Safety Codes and Standards information](https://www.esafety.gov.au/industry/codes). A classification category does not mean that material is permitted on our service. Our Terms of Service and content policies determine what users may upload or share, subject to applicable law.

**How we protect children and moderate content**

Australian users can only access pornographic material on our service using an eligible Apple device running iOS 27 or iPadOS 27, after Apple has confirmed they are aged 18 or over and our access requirements have been met. Other users are limited to a non-pornographic environment. We test and monitor the effectiveness of these protections over time.

Parents and carers can find guidance on restricting access through device settings and filtering tools on our [Parental Controls page](https://help.pornhub.com/hc/en-us/articles/4419885579795-Parental-Controls). Further information is available on our [RTA Compliance page](https://help.pornhub.com/hc/en-us/sections/4419828680851-RTA-Restricted-to-Adults-Compliance).

We require uploader identity verification and evidence of consent before publication. We prohibit child sexual exploitation material and other content that breaches our Terms of Service or content policies. We review reports and take appropriate action, which may include removing content and restricting or terminating accounts.

**Reporting Content**

You can report suspected Class 1A or Class 1B material, or Class 1C or Class 2 material that you believe breaches our Terms of Service or content policies, using our [Content Removal Request form](https://www.pornhub.com/content-removal) or the reporting tools available on the service.

To use the Content Removal Request form, open the link above, provide the content URL, explain why you believe the content breaches our rules, complete the relevant fields and submit the form. You do not need to identify the correct legal classification to make a report. We assess reports and take action where appropriate. If you are dissatisfied with the outcome or handling of your report, you can request further investigation by replying to the email from our support team.

**Complaints about our compliance**

To complain about our compliance with applicable Online Safety Codes and Standards, including the Designated Internet Services Code for Class 1C and Class 2 material, email [eSafety@pornhub.com](mailto:eSafety@pornhub.com). Please explain what you believe we have failed to do and include relevant URLs, dates and any previous correspondence or report reference. You may use this route to raise concerns about our age-assurance or access controls.

For content reports and Code-compliance complaints, we do not make your identity accessible to other users or account holders without your express consent, except where required by law.

**Unresolved complaints and referral to eSafety**

If you remain dissatisfied with the outcome or handling of a Class 1A or Class 1B report, or a complaint about our compliance with applicable Online Safety Codes and Standards, email [eSafety@pornhub.com](mailto:eSafety@pornhub.com) and we will refer your complaint to eSafety.

We will also promptly refer complaints from Australian users concerning material non-compliance with the Class 1C and Class 2 DIS Code where we are unable to resolve them within a reasonable timeframe. You do not need to make a further request for that referral.

You may complain directly to eSafety about possible non-compliance through its [Online Safety Codes and Standards complaints page](https://www.esafety.gov.au/industry/codes/complaints). To report illegal or restricted content or online abuse, visit [eSafety’s reporting page](https://www.esafety.gov.au/report). eSafety assesses regulatory compliance but does not use Code complaints to resolve individual disputes between users and services.

**Counselling and support**

If online content or an experience online has affected you, independent support is available:

*   Lifeline — crisis support on 13 11 14, with online contact options on its website.
*   1800RESPECT — counselling, information and support for domestic, family and sexual violence on 1800 737 732, with online contact options on its website.

For further options, including support for young people and other specialist services, visit [eSafety’s counselling and support directory](https://www.esafety.gov.au/about-us/counselling-support-services).

If someone is in immediate danger in Australia, call 000.
